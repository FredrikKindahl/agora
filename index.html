AGORA
The Global Voice Gap Project

Making the distance between people's will and political action impossible to ignore.

A transparent, decentralized platform where anyone can vote on policy questions, see real-time results globally, and compare them directly to how their actual representatives vote. The gap becomes visible, measurable, and undeniable.

The Vision

Democracy is built on the principle that representatives listen to the people. But when the gap between what people want and what politicians do is invisible, accountability disappears.

AGORA makes this gap visible. Not to create conflict, but to create accountability. When everyone can see the difference between public will and actual policy, it becomes impossible to ignore.

This is a tool for democratic empowerment. For activists. For journalists. For citizens who want their voice heard. For a world where those in power actually represent those they claim to serve.
