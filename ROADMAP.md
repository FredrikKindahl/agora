**Last Updated**: February 13, 2026

## Phase 3: Decentralization (October 2026 - March 2027)

**Goal**: Make AGORA censorship-resistant and truly distributed

### Technical Implementation
- [ ] Decentralized architecture
  - [ ] Evaluate: Holochain vs IPFS vs Ceramic
  - [ ] Migrate core data to distributed ledger
  - [ ] Peer-to-peer hosting options
- [ ] Enhanced verification
  - [ ] Zero-knowledge proofs for votes
  - [ ] Anti-bot measures
  - [ ] One person = one vote without revealing identity
- [ ] Encrypted communication
  - [ ] Signal integration
  - [ ] Tor hidden service
  - [ ] Works in censored environments
- [ ] Community governance
  - [ ] DAO for decision-making?
  - [ ] Token-free governance (avoid financialization)
  - [ ] Contributor voting on major changes

### Success Metrics
- 100,000+ votes cast
- 25+ countries tracked
- Works in 3+ countries with internet censorship
- No single point of failure

## Long-Term Vision (2028+)

### Ultimate Goals
1. **Universal Coverage**: Every democracy tracked
2. **Real-Time**: Live comparison during votes
3. **Impact**: Measurable policy changes from visibility
4. **Standard**: AGORA becomes default way to check gaps
5. **Self-Sustaining**: Community maintains without founders

### Potential Features
- AI-powered analysis of voting patterns
- Prediction markets for upcoming votes
- Direct democracy experiments
- Local government tracking (city councils, etc.)
- Corporate governance tracking (shareholder vs board)

---

## Risks & Challenges

### Technical
- **Scaling**: How to handle millions of votes?
- **Security**: Preventing vote manipulation
- **Data quality**: Ensuring parliamentary data accuracy
- **Uptime**: Maintaining availability

### Social
- **Adoption**: Getting people to use it
- **Trust**: Building credibility
- **Manipulation**: Preventing coordinated attacks
- **Political pressure**: Resistance from those exposed

### Legal
- **Data laws**: Compliance across countries
- **Scraping**: Legal status of parliamentary data scraping
- **Liability**: Legal protection for platform

### Financial
- **Sustainability**: Funding development without ads/data selling
- **Resources**: Server costs as we scale
